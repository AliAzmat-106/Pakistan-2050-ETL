Method

The pipeline behind this page.

01

Extract

World Bank Open Data API with Python requests. Six indicators, six economies, 2000 to latest year.

02

Transform

Pydantic validates every record. Pandas cleans, fills inner gaps and derives growth and PPP ratios.

03

Load

SQLite database (no server, one portable file) plus a CSV backup.

04

EDA

Trends, growth vs population, nominal vs PPP, country comparison and 2050 scenarios.

Why the World Bank API?

It is free, official and consistent across countries, and it directly supports the question about growth, population and nominal vs PPP comparison.

Data: World Bank Open Data. Scenarios are illustrative, not forecasts.
